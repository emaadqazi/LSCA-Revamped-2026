The Supply Chain Department is introducing an optional case competition in BU275 and BU375 for bonus marks on the topic of US tariffs.

Instead of a slide deck, participants present a poster solution, similar to World of Opportunities in fourth year. Cases are distributed about two weeks before the presentation date.

Organized by Dr. Michael Haughton, our faculty advisor, in collaboration with LSCA.
